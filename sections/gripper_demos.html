Single-arm manipulation

One right-hand controller drives the E6 and its gripper. The index trigger sets the jaw opening; the middle-finger Grip engages arm following.

Three-wire positional servo driven by an Arduino Uno. The videos show physical manipulation; the SDK reports commanded opening and serial acknowledgements. Actual jaw position is unmeasured. Control changes and setup
